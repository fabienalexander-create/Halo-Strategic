The Problem

Every agency sees the problem through the lens of what it sells.

If you build websites, every problem starts looking like a website. If you run paid ads, every problem looks like more traffic. If you sell SEO, every problem becomes an SEO opportunity.

This isn't dishonest. It's human nature.

Halo exists to diagnose the commercial problem before recommending implementation, so the recommendation your client hears is shaped by evidence, not by what happens to be on your rate card.

FAQ

What agencies ask before partnering.

Will Halo sell to my clients?

No. We don't market directly to a referred client without your agreement, and the client relationship stays yours throughout.

Can the Diagnostic be white-labelled?

Not yet. White-labelling is a planned future addition to the partner programme. Today, Diagnostics are delivered as a clearly credited part of your engagement, positioned as your strategic partner's work.

Can we co-brand reports?

Not yet. Co-branded reporting is planned for a future phase of the partner programme, not available today.

Do you replace our strategy team?

No. Halo exists to strengthen your strategic offering, not replace it. Where you already have strategic capability, we work alongside it.

What happens if marketing isn't the answer?

We tell you and your client that directly. Diagnosis before prescription means the Diagnostic follows the evidence, even when it points away from a marketing solution.

How does pricing work?

Partner terms are agreed directly during your Partner Strategy Call, since they depend on your agency's referral volume and how you want to structure client engagements.
