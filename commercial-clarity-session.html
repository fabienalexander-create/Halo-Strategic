A Focused Entry Point

The Commercial Clarity Session.

Not every business is ready for a full commercial investigation. Sometimes the immediate priority is understanding one specific constraint and deciding what to do next. That's what this session is for.

Request Your Commercial Clarity Session - $199

Need a deeper, full-journey investigation instead? See the Commercial Diagnostic →

What it is

One problem, a focused session, a clear next step.

The Commercial Clarity Session is a narrower, lower-commitment way to experience Halo's diagnostic approach. You bring one clearly defined commercial problem. We spend 45-60 minutes on it directly, then give you a preliminary read and three prioritised actions, in writing.

  • A short pre-session questionnaire
  • Review of the one commercial challenge you bring
  • A 45-60 minute focused strategy session
  • A preliminary read on the likely constraint, based on what's discussed
  • Three prioritised recommended actions
  • A concise written summary after the session

This is intentionally narrower than the Commercial Diagnostic. It is not a full commercial audit, an extensive multi-channel data review, an implementation engagement, or ongoing advisory. If your situation turns out to need that depth, we'll tell you directly during or after the session.

Who this is for

A good fit if you already know the question.

The Clarity Session suits a business owner or decision-maker who:

  • Has one specific commercial problem they want a second, expert opinion on
  • Isn't yet ready to commit to a full diagnostic investigation
  • Wants a low-risk way to see how Halo actually thinks before going deeper
  • Needs a prioritised next step, not a lengthy report

If your situation spans multiple departments, or you're not sure yet what the real question even is, the Commercial Diagnostic or the free Commercial Health Check are better starting points.

How is this different from the $995 Commercial Diagnostic?

The Clarity Session looks at one defined problem you bring to it. The Diagnostic is a broader, structured 90-minute investigation across your whole commercial journey. If you already know the single issue you want clarity on, start here. If you want a fuller picture of where your business is leaking growth, start with the Diagnostic.

Is the $199 session a cheaper version of the Diagnostic?

No. It is a separate, narrower service, not a discounted Diagnostic. It intentionally does not include the Diagnostic's full-journey review, and does not come with the Halo Clarity Guarantee, which is specific to the Diagnostic.

What if my problem turns out to be bigger than one session can cover?

We'll tell you directly, during or after the session, and explain why, rather than stretching the session to cover something it wasn't built for. The natural next step from there is the Commercial Diagnostic.
