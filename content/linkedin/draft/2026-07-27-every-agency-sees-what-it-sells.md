## LinkedIn copy

Every agency tends to see a client's problem through the lens of what it sells. An SEO agency finds an SEO problem. A paid-media agency finds a paid-media problem.

Not dishonesty — just how specialisation narrows a diagnosis by default.

A growing number of agencies are starting to close that gap by partnering with an independent commercial diagnostic instead of stretching their own service to cover it. The agency keeps the relationship and the work. The diagnosis just isn't coming from whoever's about to sell the fix.

None of this requires giving up the work. It requires being honest about which part of the problem is diagnosis, and which part is execution.

More on how this works: https://halostrategic.com/agency-partnerships
