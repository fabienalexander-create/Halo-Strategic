## LinkedIn copy

Business maturity has nothing to do with revenue.

A £300k business can be highly mature, if its decisions are disciplined and evidence-based. A £20m business can still be reactive, constantly chasing symptoms instead of understanding the constraint underneath them.

Five levels, roughly:

Reactive — the founder runs everything, no systems, constant firefighting.
Aware — recurring problems get noticed, but not yet understood.
Diagnosing — "what evidence tells us that's the real constraint," not "how do we fix it."
Optimising — decisions are already evidence-based; now it's about refining continuously.
Aligned — every function pulls toward the same outcome, and growth compounds instead of just happening.

Which one sounds like where your business actually is right now, not where the org chart says it should be?
