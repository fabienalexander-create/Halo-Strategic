## LinkedIn copy

A client wasn't unhappy with performance. They were unhappy that they couldn't see it.

The account was sitting inside its agreed guardrail the entire time it was being escalated as underperforming. Reporting was too dense to act on, there was no regular cadence of contact, and no way to tell if the account was actually growing over time.

Once "the client is unhappy" got separated from "the account is underperforming" — two different facts, easily confused — the fix wasn't a campaign change. It was designing for visibility as a deliverable in its own right.

Good reporting changes decisions. Dense reporting just changes who has to defend themselves in the next call.
