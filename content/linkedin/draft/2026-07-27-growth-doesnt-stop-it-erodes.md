## LinkedIn copy

Businesses rarely fail because of one mistake. They fail because small problems are allowed to compound.

A lead waits an extra day for a callback. A proposal sits unopened. A customer leaves and nobody asks why. None of these look significant on their own — that's exactly what makes them dangerous.

The instinct is to look for the next opportunity. The better question is usually what's quietly preventing the business that already exists from performing the way it should.

Most quarters don't collapse. They erode, one unremarkable Tuesday at a time.
