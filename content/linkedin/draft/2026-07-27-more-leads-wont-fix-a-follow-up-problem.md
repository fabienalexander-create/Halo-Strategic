## LinkedIn copy

Every growth conversation starts the same way: "we need more leads."

Sometimes true. Often not.

One business we worked with needed to lift site visits from three a week to six or eight. The obvious move was more marketing spend.

Then someone actually looked at what happened after a lead arrived. Fourteen leads were producing one booked visit. Nearly half of those leads were outside the service area entirely.

Buying more leads for a business that isn't following up on the ones it already has is a bit like ordering more coffee because the first cup went cold. More of the same problem, just bigger.

The fix wasn't a bigger ad budget. It was fixing what happened in the first hour after a lead landed.

Before asking for more leads, it's worth asking what's happening to the ones already arriving.
