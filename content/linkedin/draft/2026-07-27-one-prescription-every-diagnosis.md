## LinkedIn copy

When an agency's revenue depends on selling SEO, its diagnosis tends to conclude the business needs more SEO. When it depends on paid ads, the diagnosis leans there instead.

That's not usually dishonesty. It's an incentive quietly shaping a conclusion before the diagnosis has even started.

Imagine a doctor who only ever prescribed the one medication their practice happened to sell, regardless of the actual diagnosis. Everyone would recognise something was wrong immediately.

In marketing, it's treated as completely normal.

More traffic, more followers, more leads — none of it is evidence a business is actually growing, if the person reporting the number is also the one being paid to move it.

Ask who benefits most from the metric being watched, before trusting what it's telling you.
