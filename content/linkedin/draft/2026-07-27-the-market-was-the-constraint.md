## LinkedIn copy

Spend was going out. Almost no leads were coming back. The client was ready to pause billing.

On the surface, a campaign failure — wrong targeting, weak creative, an execution problem to fix.

It wasn't. The niche simply didn't generate enough search volume to justify the budget behind it. Roughly a quarter of the monthly spend could actually be deployed; the rest had nowhere to go. A tracking bug was also quietly inflating the conversion numbers, making a weak channel look like a promising one.

No amount of campaign optimisation fixes a channel that structurally can't spend what's behind it. The honest answer wasn't "try harder." It was recognising a real demand-side ceiling and changing channel entirely.

Sometimes the market is the constraint, not the marketing.
