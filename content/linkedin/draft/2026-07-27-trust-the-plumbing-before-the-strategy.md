## LinkedIn copy

A client's agency relationship had narrowed to "launch campaigns, react to issues." Underperformance in a newly launched market looked like ordinary growing pains — the kind more spend usually fixes.

It wasn't. Tracking parameters were passing placeholder values. Form-to-CRM data was inconsistent. Scripts were quietly interfering with attribution.

The real fault wasn't the campaign. It was whether the numbers could be trusted at all.

A performance problem and a trust-in-the-data problem look identical from the outside. The only way to tell them apart is checking the plumbing before touching the strategy.

Fixing tracking first, before any performance call was made, is what actually moved this account from reacting to leading.
