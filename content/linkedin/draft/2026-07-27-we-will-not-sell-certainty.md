## LinkedIn copy

We will not sell certainty where certainty doesn't exist.

Halo won't manufacture confidence simply because confidence is easier to sell than honesty. If the evidence says "we're not sure yet," that's what gets told to a client — not a story dressed up to sound more finished than it actually is.

It's a slower way to build trust. It's also the only way that survives being wrong occasionally, which every diagnosis eventually is.
