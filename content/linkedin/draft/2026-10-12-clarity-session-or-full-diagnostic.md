## LinkedIn copy

"Should we do the full Diagnostic, or just get a quick opinion first?" is a fair question, and the honest answer depends on what you already know.

A focused Clarity Session fits when you already know the question. One specific problem, clearly stated, that you want an expert pair of eyes on before deciding what to do next.

A full Diagnostic fits when you don't yet know where the problem actually sits. Leads, sales, pricing, positioning, tracking, something in between, and you need the whole journey examined before trusting any one answer.

Neither is the "serious" option and the other the "lite" one. They solve different problems. Buying the wrong depth either wastes money on more investigation than the question needed, or gives you an answer that's too narrow to trust.

Worth asking honestly before booking either one: do I know the specific question, or am I still looking for it?
