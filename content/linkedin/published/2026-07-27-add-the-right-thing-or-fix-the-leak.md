## LinkedIn copy

Ask a founder what growth requires and the answer is almost always the same shape: a new agency, a new CRM, a new hire, a new tool.

Add the right thing, and growth follows. Sometimes true. Most of the time, it isn't.

Growth doesn't usually stall because a business lacks another tool. It stalls because the business has stopped converting the potential it already has — leads going unanswered, customers not coming back, data collected and never used.

There's little point pouring more water into a bucket that's still leaking.

Before adding the next thing: where is the business already losing value it hasn't noticed yet?
