## LinkedIn copy

Not every business needs a full commercial investigation to take the next step.

Sometimes the immediate priority is understanding one specific constraint, and deciding what to do about it. Nothing more, nothing less.

That's what the Commercial Clarity Session is for: a focused 45-60 minute session on one commercial problem you bring, a preliminary read on the likely constraint, three prioritised actions, and a written summary. $199.

It isn't a cheaper version of the Commercial Diagnostic. It's a separate, narrower service, built for a specific moment: you know the question, you just want an expert pair of eyes on it before deciding what's next.

If your problem turns out to need a fuller investigation, we'll tell you directly, and the Diagnostic is the natural next step from there.
