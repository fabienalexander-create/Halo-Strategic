## The Halo Clarity Guarantee

> If you leave your Commercial Diagnostic without significantly more clarity about what's holding your business back and what should happen next, we'll refund your investment in full.

Kept close to the working version, tightened slightly for precision:

> **The Halo Clarity Guarantee.** Every Commercial Diagnostic is built to leave you with real clarity: what's actually holding your business back, and what should happen next. If it doesn't, tell us, and we'll refund it in full.

## Customer promise

The guarantee is about clarity, not results. It commits Halo to a standard of diagnostic quality, not to a business outcome. This distinction has to survive every rewrite of this copy:

- **Is:** "you'll understand your business's constraint better after this session than before it."
- **Is not:** a revenue guarantee, an ROI guarantee, or a growth guarantee.

This is consistent with the Philosophy's Chapter 2 ("we won't manufacture confidence simply because confidence is easier to sell than honesty") and Chapter 6 ("we will not sell certainty where certainty doesn't exist"). A results guarantee would directly contradict both; a clarity guarantee doesn't, because clarity is something Halo controls and can be held to, a client's revenue outcome isn't.

## Legal and commercial considerations (flag, not advice)

This document is not a substitute for review by a solicitor or accountant, and shouldn't be published as-is without one:

- **Refund trigger is subjective.** "Significantly more clarity" is a judgement call, not a measurable condition. Needs a decision, before publishing, on what happens when a client asks for the refund: granted on request, no questions asked, or a review step? The copy below assumes the former, since a review step undercuts the guarantee's own promise of confidence, but this is a real operational decision, not just wording.
- **Time window.** No expiry is specified. A refund request six months after the session is a different commitment than one made within a week. Needs a stated window.
- **Cross-border payments.** If the Diagnostic is sold in USD to US clients (see `PRICING_STRATEGY.md`) while Halo remains a UK entity, refund processing involves currency conversion and cross-border payment handling, worth confirming with whoever handles payments before this is live.
- **Consumer protection law.** Refund guarantees for services can intersect with consumer protection regulation differently in the UK vs. US, and differently again depending on whether the buyer is a business or an individual. This needs a solicitor's confirmation, not an assumption baked into marketing copy.

## FAQ

**Does this guarantee apply to the Commercial Audit or other products?**
As currently scoped, no, only the Commercial Diagnostic. The Audit is a longer, deeper engagement where "clarity" as a single yes/no outcome is a harder promise to make cleanly; extending the guarantee further is a separate decision.

**What if I'm not sure whether I got enough clarity?**
[Depends on the review-step decision above. If on-request: "If you're not sure, that's reason enough to ask, we'd rather refund a Diagnostic than have you walk away unconvinced."]

**How long do I have to request a refund?**
[Open, needs a decided window, suggested placeholder: 14 days from the session.]

**Is this a money-back guarantee on results?**
No. Explicitly not. See the Customer Promise section above, this is worth keeping in the FAQ verbatim so it's never ambiguous in front of a client.
