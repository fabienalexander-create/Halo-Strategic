# Buyer Discovery — Shared Core Questions

Status: internal reference only, not published. This is the backbone both
vertical discovery guides (`hvac-buyer-discovery-questionnaire-DRAFT.md` and
`financial-property-buyer-discovery-questionnaire-DRAFT.md`) are built on,
so answers from Hypothesis A (HVAC) and Hypothesis B (Financial & Property)
can be compared directly instead of being two unrelated sets of findings.

Each vertical guide asks these same ten questions, worded for that
vertical's context, in the same order, plus a small number of
vertical-specific questions where genuinely needed (e.g. HVAC service
radius, Financial/Property regulatory status). Do not let the
vertical-specific additions replace or reorder the core ten — the point is
comparability.

## The ten core questions

1. What are you currently doing to acquire customers?
2. What does a good opportunity look like to you?
3. What makes you reject one?
4. What do you currently pay (per lead, per opportunity, per acquisition)?
5. What is the approximate value of a new customer to you?
6. How quickly do you follow up once an opportunity arrives?
7. Where does your current process break down?
8. What would make you switch supplier?
9. Would you buy qualified opportunities on a pay-per-lead basis?
10. What would you need to see before testing a new source?

## Why these ten, specifically

They map directly onto the two things this whole validation phase exists to
answer, for each hypothesis independently:

- **Is there a repeatable commercial model here?** (questions 1, 4, 5, 9 —
  acquisition cost, value, and willingness to pay for opportunities)
- **Is there a real qualification gap Halo can close?** (questions 2, 3, 6,
  7 — what "good" means to the buyer, where their process actually breaks)
- **Is there room for Halo to win the business?** (questions 8, 10 —
  switching triggers and proof requirements)

## How to use this across both hypotheses

After each hypothesis has a handful of completed conversations, compare
answers to the same-numbered question side by side — HVAC's answer to
question 9 next to Financial/Property's answer to question 9, and so on.
That comparison, not either vertical's numbers in isolation, is what tells
Fabien where Halo's Lead Network actually has an advantage.

Do not average or combine the two verticals' answers. They are separate
hypotheses until the evidence says otherwise.
