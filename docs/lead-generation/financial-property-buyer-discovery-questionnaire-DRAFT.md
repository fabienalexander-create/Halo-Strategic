# Financial & Property Buyer Discovery Questionnaire — DRAFT, INTERNAL ONLY

Status: not published, not a contract, not a script to read verbatim. This
is Hypothesis B's discovery guide, built on the same ten core questions as
the HVAC guide (`buyer-discovery-core-questions.md`) so the two markets can
be compared directly. Adapt the wording to how the conversation actually
goes; keep the underlying questions intact.

**Scope for this round: ordinary professional services only** — accounting,
bookkeeping, financial planning, property services (letting/sales agencies,
property management). Per the Financial & Property Vertical Strategy Brief,
do NOT use this questionnaire to solicit or discuss regulated financial
products (investment products, trading platforms, wealth management,
mortgages) — that category needs a compliance opinion before any buyer
conversation happens, let alone a commercial one.

## Before the call

Confirm: business name, service category (accounting / bookkeeping /
financial planning / property letting / property management / other —
record exactly what they do), city or region, how the contact was sourced.

If at any point the conversation reveals the business is itself a
regulated financial-product provider (not just a professional-services
firm), stop and flag it to Fabien rather than continuing the discovery
questions — this guide is scoped to non-regulated services only.

## Current state

1. What are you currently doing to acquire new clients (referrals, paid
   ads, SEO, partnerships, cold outreach, other)?
2. What does a good new-client opportunity look like to you — what makes
   someone worth your time versus not?
3. What makes you turn away or disqualify a prospective client you've
   already spoken to?
4. What do you currently pay to acquire a client, if anything (per lead,
   per referral fee, marketing spend per client, or "nothing, it's all
   referral")?
5. What is a new client roughly worth to you — first-year value or
   typical engagement value, rough is fine?
6. How quickly do you follow up once a new enquiry comes in?
7. Where does your current client-acquisition process actually break down
   — too few enquiries, enquiries that go nowhere, slow follow-up,
   something else?
8. What would make you switch to, or add, a new source of client
   opportunities?
9. Would you consider paying for qualified client opportunities on a
   pay-per-lead or pay-per-opportunity basis?
10. What would you need to see — proof, a trial, references — before
    testing a new source?

## The pricing question — ask this exact way

> "If I could consistently send you the type of enquiry you actually
> want, what would you consider a fair price per opportunity?"

Do not suggest a number first. Do not react to their number, good or bad —
just record it. The goal is market signal, not negotiation, on this call.

## Close

- Would they be open to a small trial batch once Halo has a defined
  qualification process for this vertical?
- Best way to follow up, and expected timeline for them to actually decide.

## After the call

Record raw answers against the same ten core-question numbers used in the
HVAC guide, so the two hypotheses line up question-for-question. Don't
summarize or interpret yet — raw answers first, patterns come later once
there are several conversations, in both verticals, to compare.
