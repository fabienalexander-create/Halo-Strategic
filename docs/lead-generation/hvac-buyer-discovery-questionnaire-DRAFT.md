# HVAC Buyer Discovery Questionnaire — DRAFT, INTERNAL ONLY

Status: not published, not a contract, not a script to read verbatim. A
structured guide so the first 5 HVAC buyer conversations produce comparable
answers instead of five different unstructured chats. Adapt the wording to
how the conversation actually goes; keep the underlying questions intact so
the answers can go straight into the Buyer Pipeline tracker.

## Before the call

Confirm: business name, HVAC segment (install/repair/maintenance/all), city
or region, how the contact was sourced.

## Current state

1. Do you currently buy leads? If yes, from whom?
2. Where do those leads come from (Google, Facebook, Angi/HomeAdvisor,
   referral networks, other)?
3. What do you currently pay per lead, or per job booked?
4. What makes you reject a lead you've paid for?
5. What makes a lead valuable to you, specifically — what does a "good"
   lead look like versus a "bad" one you've received before?
6. How quickly can your team respond to a new lead once it arrives?
7. What geographic area do you actually service (be specific — zip codes
   or a radius, not just "the region")?
8. Which HVAC jobs do you actually want more of? Which ones aren't worth
   it to you?
9. What does a typical booked job mean economically for you — roughly,
   not precisely (e.g. "most installs are worth X to us")?
10. Would you consider pay-per-lead from a new supplier, and what would
    make you trust a new source enough to try it?

## The pricing question — ask this exact way

> "If I could consistently send you the type of enquiry you actually
> want, what would you consider a fair price per lead?"

Do not suggest a number first. Do not react to their number, good or bad —
just record it. The goal is market signal, not negotiation, on this call.

## Close

- Would they be open to a small trial batch once Halo has a defined
  qualification process?
- Best way to follow up, and expected timeline for them to actually decide.

## After the call

Transfer every answer straight into a new row in
`hvac-buyer-pipeline.md`. Don't summarize or interpret yet — raw answers
first, patterns come later once there are several rows to compare.
