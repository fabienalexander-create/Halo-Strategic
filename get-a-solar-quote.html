Solar Panels

Solar panels, explained simply.

Solar panels convert daylight into electricity for your home, which can reduce how much you draw from the grid while the sun is out. How much a property benefits depends on factors such as roof orientation, shading, pitch and your household's electricity usage — which is why an installer will usually want to assess your specific property before quoting, rather than giving a generic figure.

Battery Storage

Battery storage, explained simply.

A home battery stores electricity — generated by solar panels, or bought from the grid at cheaper times — for use later, such as in the evening or overnight. Batteries can be fitted alongside solar panels or, in some cases, on their own. Whether one makes sense for your property depends on your usage pattern and existing setup, which again is something an installer should assess directly rather than estimate generically.

Why Homeowners Explore This

Common reasons people look into solar and battery.

  • Wanting more control over rising electricity costs.
  • Interest in using more home-generated electricity, and storing it for later use.
  • Planning other home improvements and wanting to understand options at the same time.
  • Simply wanting a professional opinion on whether their specific property is suitable.

We don't promise a specific saving figure for your home — that depends on your property, usage and the quote an installer gives you, and any installer worth using will say the same.

Before You Enquire

Frequently asked questions.

Is this a quote?

No. This is a short enquiry to establish whether it's worth putting you in touch with an installation company. Any quote or survey comes later, directly from the installer, and is not something Halo Strategic provides.

How much does installation cost?

It varies by property, system size and installer, so we can't give a figure here. An installer will quote based on a proper assessment of your home.

How do you know if my property is suitable?

We can't tell you that for certain from a short form — only a site assessment can confirm suitability. What we do is check your answers against basic criteria before passing your enquiry on, so you're not introduced to an installer for no reason.

Do batteries work without solar panels?

In some setups, yes. Whether it's worthwhile for your home depends on your usage and tariff, which is something to discuss with an installer directly.

Is finance available?

Finance options, where available, are offered by the installation company, not by Halo Strategic. If relevant to you, ask the installer directly once you're introduced.

What happens after I submit the form?

We review your answers against basic suitability criteria. If it looks like a reasonable fit, we introduce you to an appropriate installation company, who may then contact you directly to arrange a survey or quote. If it doesn't look like a fit, we'll let you know rather than pass your details on anyway.

Who will contact me, and why?

Halo Strategic may contact you about this enquiry. If your enquiry is passed on, the installation company you're introduced to may also contact you, to discuss your enquiry, arrange a survey or provide a quote. See the privacy and consent section below for more detail.

Does Halo Strategic install the panels?

No. Halo Strategic is not an installer. We help connect homeowner enquiries with installation companies; any installation itself is carried out by that company, not by us.
