How we turn genuine homeowner interest into a qualified, consented enquiry for your business — and why you only ever pay for what's been verified.

01
ENQUIRY
A homeowner responds to an advert or submits an enquiry through a website form — this is genuine, self-reported demand, not a cold list.
Homeowner details and the original enquiry are captured at source.
02
QUALIFICATION
We check the information given and follow up for any missing detail — property type, timeframe, and what the homeowner actually needs.
Consent and eligibility are confirmed before a lead can progress any further.
03
INSTALLER MATCH
The lead is matched against service type and postcode coverage, so it only ever reaches businesses genuinely able to take it on.
Only relevant, eligible buyers are considered — never a blanket broadcast.
04
PAYMENT & RELEASE
You review a redacted summary first, and pay only once you're satisfied it meets the criteria agreed between us.
Full contact details are released only after payment is verified.
