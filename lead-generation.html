The Belief Behind It

A lead is an input into the commercial system, not the outcome.

You can generate a thousand leads and still have a broken commercial system. You can have excellent leads and terrible follow-up. You can have cheap leads that produce poor customers, or expensive leads that produce highly profitable ones.

The value of a lead depends on quality, qualification, conversion, economics, and what happens after the enquiry, not the raw count.

That's the same diagnosis-first thinking behind every Halo engagement, applied to lead generation instead of treated as a separate discipline. It's also the subject of a Founder Perspective piece worth reading before you buy leads from anyone, including Halo: What Counts as a Qualified Lead Isn't Universal →

Before You Get in Touch

What people ask first.

How is this different from a normal lead-gen agency?

Most lead-gen arrangements are paid on volume, which rewards quantity over fit. Halo's pay-per-lead model is paid on an agreed definition of quality, decided before any campaign launches, not after.

What counts as a qualified lead?

Whatever matters most to your business, agreed before any campaign launches: service area, project type, and any other signal you define. Nothing is decided after the fact.

Does this compete with Halo's Commercial Diagnostic?

No. Lead Generation is a separate commercial pathway for businesses that already know they want more qualified opportunities. The Diagnostic is for businesses that first need to understand what's actually constraining growth.

What industries does Halo generate leads for?

Currently HVAC and roofing, drawing on real home-improvement lead-generation experience. If your industry isn't listed, get in touch, it may still be a fit.

Ready to talk about qualified opportunities?

A direct conversation about your service area, project types, and what "qualified" should mean for your business.
