How Halo's Products Fit Together

Clarity → Strategy → Transformation → Partnership.

Halo's products aren't a list of services to choose from. They're stages in one journey: understanding what's actually happening in your business, then fixing it in the right order. Every stage below follows Halo's First Law: diagnosis before prescription, always.

Every engagement begins with diagnosis. Not every client needs a Commercial Audit. Not every Commercial Audit leads to a Transformation project. And not every Transformation requires an ongoing Strategic Partnership. Our responsibility is to recommend what is genuinely in your business's best interests, not to move you through a predefined sales journey.

You don't need to start at the top. Most businesses start with the free Commercial Health Check or Commercial Diagnostic, and only move further once a real constraint has actually been identified, not before. No stage below is sold on its own merits, independent of the diagnosis that precedes it.
