Before You Talk to Us

What installers usually ask.

What exactly do you supply?

Homeowner enquiries for residential solar panel and battery installers, targeted to your agreed postcodes and checked against criteria we agree with you.

How are leads qualified?

We agree the criteria before the pilot, such as location, property type and project interest. We're clear about which details are collected, which are checked, and what counts as a valid lead.

Are leads exclusive?

We'll agree this upfront. Your pilot terms will state whether leads are sent only to you or shared.

How quickly are leads delivered?

Leads are sent to you by email as they come in. Timing targets are confirmed in your pilot terms.

What if a lead is invalid?

Your pilot terms will set out exactly what counts as invalid, and what happens when you report one.

Can I pause?

Yes. Your pilot terms will set out the notice period.

How much do leads cost?

You pay per lead, at a price agreed before the pilot starts. There's no retainer.

Do you guarantee sales?

No. We generate enquiries that match your criteria. Turning them into installs depends on contact speed, pricing and your sales process.
